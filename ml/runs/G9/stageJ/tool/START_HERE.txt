NutriSole photo labelling - __BATCH__ - copy: __COPY__  (__N__ photos)
=====================================================================

1. Unzip this folder anywhere (Desktop is fine).
2. Double-click the .html file. It opens in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. No internet needed.
3. Type your name, read the 2-minute guide (the Help button), then label:
      keys 1-9 choose the answer, arrow keys move, C/B/M/X add tags, U = next unlabelled.
   Your work saves itself in that browser after every click. You can close it and come back later
   (same computer, same browser). Do not clear the browser history before exporting.
4. When finished (or at the end of each session) press "Export CSV" and send the downloaded .csv
   file to the project lead (WhatsApp / email is fine).

Rules: label on your own, do not discuss photos with the other labeller until both of you have exported,
and choose 9 (uncertain) whenever you honestly cannot tell. Full rules: annotation_guide.md.

If the .html file will not open: use fallback/fallback_*.csv in Excel or Google Sheets and look at the photos
in fallback/contact_sheets (each photo has its id in red). Fill the "label" column with one of:
apple, banana, citrus_orange, tomato, carrot, other_produce, printed_or_screen, occluded, uncertain
