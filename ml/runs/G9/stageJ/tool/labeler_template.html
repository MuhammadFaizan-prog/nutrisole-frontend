Quick guide (2 minutes)

For each photo, pick one answer for the main item in the centre. Go with your first honest impression; do not search the web.

1 appleAny whole apple: red, green, yellow, with spots or bruises.
2 bananaA banana or a bunch of bananas, green to brown. Plantain counts as banana only if you are sure; else 9.
3 citrus_orangeOrange, kinnow, malta, mandarin, tangerine, clementine. Not lemon or lime (those are 6), not grapefruit or pomelo (6).
4 tomatoA tomato, red or green, any size incl. cherry tomatoes.
5 carrotAn orange (or purple) carrot root. Carrot leaves alone are not enough.
6 other_produceA real fruit or vegetable that is none of the above: lemon, lime, mango, guava, pomegranate, potato, onion, capsicum (bell pepper), chili, peach, persimmon... Pick its name in the list if you can.
7 printed_or_screenNot real produce: a picture, poster, screen, drawing, painting, plastic or toy fruit, a sign, a map.
8 occludedReal produce, but less than about half of the main item is visible: hidden by a hand, cloth, other fruit, the photo edge, or a bag you cannot see through.
9 uncertainYou cannot tell what it is, the photo is too blurry/dark, or there is no clear main item. This is a good answer when you are unsure.

Tags: C cut, peeled or sliced · B in a bag/net/packet (still choose the fruit if you can see it clearly) · M several items / pile · X rotten or damaged.

Keys: 1-9 choose · ←/→ previous/next · U next unlabelled · N note · E export · ? this guide.
