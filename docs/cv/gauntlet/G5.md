**Bottom line.** **No G5 candidate is promoted. The G3 control (yolo11n 640 -> 480 raw+NMS + G3 E224 quality classifier, T=0.25, scene-guard rule 2 off) stays champion under both the strict and the combined view.** The detector fine-tunes (ft0 / fthn / fthnc, all fine-tuned from A1 at 480 on a 1.5-2.1k image subset) raise pooled mAP50 from 0.314 to 0.331-0.360, but they fail the strict per-class rule (tomato and banana drop on test_rf vs G3) and all three fail the video gauntlet (det segments 0.542-0.583 vs 0.667; 6.25-17.5 false alarms/min on the G1 negative clip vs 1.25; fthnc rotten segment recall 0.625 vs 0.875). ## Promotion decision
- **Strict view:** no promotion. Every fine-tune drops > 1 pt on test_rf tomato and banana vs the G3 control; ft0 is also worse on the video gauntlet.
- **Combined view:** no promotion. Pooled gains (+0.017 to +0.046) do not survive the gauntlet: ft0 loses 3 det segments and is 14x worse on G1-neg false alarms; fthn is 7x worse; fthnc (best of the three on video: 0.583) still loses 2 segments, has 5x the G1-neg false alarms, drops rotten segment recall to 0.625 and loses tomato on test_oiv7c.
