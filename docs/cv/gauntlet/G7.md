## Bottom line
- **Detector: no promotion. G3 control (yolo11n trained 640, deployed 480, raw + class-wise NMS 0.7) stays champion under both the strict and the combined view.** All three from-scratch rpx arms fail prereg rule 1 (pooled mAP50 > 0.314, n=829): sA 0.282, sB 0.286, rfs 0.311; and rule 2 (strict per-class): every arm loses test_rf banana and tomato by 0.09-0.13 mAP50 (sA/sB also apple). Rule 5 (both seeds of one recipe) also fails for sA/sB, and rfs has no second seed. No arm earned the gated gauntlet; a descriptive (non-gating) gauntlet was run for information (section 8).
- What the new data *did* fix: **lemon vs citrus**. On test_oiv7c (n=181) the lemon->citrus wrong-label rate falls from 0.658 (G3) to 0.20-0.21 (sA/sB), and test_oiv7c mAP50 rises 0.634 -> 0.67-0.77. **rfs** (repeat-factor sampling + G3 apple/tomato floor) is the best arm: equal to G3 on `test` (0.224) and `test_small6` (0.248 vs 0.249), test_rf apple 0.27 -> 0.35, but banana and tomato still regress.
- **Quality classifier, scene guard, rubric, tracker default: unchanged.** ByteTrack b15 passes the post-hoc movable rule (exploratory) and BoT-SORT passed its DEV gate; both stay eval-only, ByteTrack is the recommended phone / labelled-footage A/B candidate.
- **Ripeness: per-fruit head trained and wired OFF by default** (passes its wire gate: test_cc0 stage acc among assessed 0.989, overripe recall 0.967, n=180; de-duplicated top-1 0.968, n=155).
- **Web video: 69 independent recordings (38 G6 + 31 new) from 44 uploaders; 0 human-verified.** Auto-labels exist only as proposals for 12 / 69 clips.
