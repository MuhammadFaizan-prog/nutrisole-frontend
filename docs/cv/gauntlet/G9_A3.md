# G9 follow-up ablation A3: G3 (0.35) + 3-frame confirmation

Date: Wed 2026-10-14 (PKT). Addendum (fixed config and limits, committed **before** any A3 number): `docs/cv/gauntlet/G9_A3_addendum.md` (commit 6929186). Parent: `G9_fp_prereg.md`, `G9_fp_stageA.md`. Data and scripts: `ml/runs/G9/fp/a3/`.
**Status: post-hoc follow-up on an already-seen holdout. NOT a preregistered confirmatory test.** A pass would only have been "provisional" pending fresh, independent footage.
Evidence labels: **sandbox CPU** = DEV replays of cached detections (2-core shared Linux). **Kaggle CPU** = holdout replay (kernel `mfaizannnn/nutrisole-g9-a3-holdout` v1, 34 clips, 1 thread per worker, ~10-12.5 fps fixed-frame, wall 1 993 s). Nothing was measured on a phone, and nothing was trained or tuned.

**A3** = G3 exactly as A0 (0.35 floor for all 12 classes, same preprocessing, NMS, area guard), with the tracker display gate **minHits 3, birth margin 0** (stock `IouTracker`: a track is shown once it has >= 3 matched boxes, the birth box included, and only on frames where it is matched).

## Bottom line
- **FAIL against the limits. No promotion; G3 stays champion.** A3 loses **1 of 22** verified pairs: **carrot #16 (ca1cbf110), a showcase class.** Limit 1 (no lost showcase pair) fails. The FA cut cannot compensate.
- **Kept / lost:** **lemon_lime #1 is kept** (6.96 s shown vs 7.92 s for A0; A1/A2 lost it). **Carrot #16 is lost again** (0.30 s shown vs the 0.5 s rule; A0 0.50 s). No other pair changes: A3 is 10/22 (A0 11, A1 9, A2 9).
- **FA reduction (holdout):** whole clip, provisional: **2.859 to 1.512 per min (-47%, 104 to 55 tracks)**. That is just short of the halving target (<= 1.43). In the unbiased **verified review-frame windows** (3.15 min): **2.86 to 0.64 per min (-78%, 9 to 2 tracks)**, the same as A2. Uploader bootstrap A3 - A0: FA/min [-2.28, -0.76], recall [-0.18, 0.0].
- **Added delay:** **+1 processed frame (+0.08 s at 12-12.5 fps) for 8 of the 10 pairs both arms detect.** Exceptions: +0.24 s (apple #1), and **+49.8 s for cd1a3f857 banana** (first display 72.8 to 122.7 s): the early banana boxes never reach 3 matches within one track. Median added delay 0.08 s. On DEV, TTFC from first visibility p50/p90 is 467/707 ms for A0 and 533/707 ms for A3 (per-segment +0 to +133 ms, which is 0-2 frames at 15 fps).
- **DEV:** A3 gives **no FA reduction** on the DEV neg clip (0.75 to 0.75 per min; all 3 DEV false tracks last >= 3 frames, though they are shorter, mean 0.20 to 0.13 s). Seg acc is unchanged (0.5417). Frame acc is 0.4365 to 0.4309. Per-class frame recall is within 1 pp (banana .627 to .620, carrot .150 to .146). So the DEV FA goal (<= 0.5 x A0) is **not met** by A3. On real footage the gate removes many more of the 1-2-frame flickers.

## 5. Processing latency (not phone)
- Detector (G3 ONNX, 480, 1 thread, Kaggle CPU, A3 run): p50 138 ms, p95 166 ms per frame (median of per-clip percentiles). Stage A run: 152 / 184 ms. A3 does not change the detector.
- Post-processing (threshold + area guard + tracker): Kaggle CPU p50 0.006 ms (A3) and 0.020 ms (A0, which runs first in the loop, so warm-up/cache order explains the gap). Sandbox CPU cached-detection replay: 0.025 ms/frame (A3). **The gate adds no compute.** Its cost is the display delay of 1-2 frames (section 2), i.e. ~0.1-0.2 s at 10 fps.

**A3 fails. No promotion, not even provisional.** The 3-frame rule stays as fixed in the addendum and is not modified after these results. A 2-of-3 rule or a crop verifier stays later DEV-only work with its own prereg and fresh footage.
What the result shows (descriptive, not confirmatory): the display gate on its own is close to free on this holdout (only 1 lost pair). That one pair is a 2.2-s clip where A0 itself sits exactly on the 0.5-s rule. The gate removes about half of whole-clip false tracks and about 78% in the unbiased windows. The high per-class thresholds (A1) are what cost lemon_lime. Any confirmation rule will keep failing the carrot #16 test as long as the scorer's 0.5-s rule is applied to a clip with 0.6 s of carrot.
